STANDALONE PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE BRIEF

What “Zero-Knowledge” Means in Clearspace

People already distrust uploading sensitive conversations to cloud AI wrappers—and they should. Clearspace is engineered so we cannot see, access, or monetize your data even if subpoenaed or breached, because your data never leaves your local disk.

01 • ZERO CLOUD STORAGE

100% On-Device SQLite Vault

When you import your OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, or Google Gemini Takeout archives, every byte is parsed directly in local RAM and persisted to clearspace_vault.db on your machine. There is no Clearspace cloud database, account login, or background sync.

02 • DETERMINISTIC PII SCRUBBER

Scrubbed Before Touching Disk

Before any prompt is indexed in your local SQLite database, our compiled Rust scrubber (crates/pii-scrubber) automatically redacts API keys (sk-..., ghp_...), emails, phone numbers, SSNs, and local filesystem paths.

03 • HUMAN PROMPTS ONLY

Assistant Replies Are Ignored

Clearspace strips out verbose AI responses, third-party code blocks, and UI noise during ingestion. Only your first-person questions, architectural inquiries, and reflections are analyzed.
