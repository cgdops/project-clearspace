How We Use AI & Deterministic Behavioral Scoring

Clearspace does not pipe your private thoughts into an external cloud LLM API. Instead, it treats the AI prompts you have already written across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini as an unmasked behavioral graph—and scores them deterministically on your CPU.

01 • EVIDENCE-WEIGHTED DEPTH RUBRIC

Zero Ego-Inflation Calibration

Every prompt is weighted by demonstrated technical and strategic depth: 0.35x for Beginner lookup, 0.75x for Intermediate component implementation, and 1.15x for Advanced systems architecture, unit-economics modeling, and adversarial verification.

02 • 3-CHAPTER NARRATIVE MIRROR

Excel, Grow & Friction Synthesis

Your prompts are organized into a 3-part story: Where You Currently Excel (demonstrated superpowers), Where We Think You Could Grow (untested assumptions & seniority gaps), and What Is Causing You Friction (repetitive maintenance traps).

03 • CITATION-BACKED O*NET 29.x MATCHING

Every Insight Cites Your Exact Words

Role fit scores across O*NET 29.x tracks are computed from Skill Overlap, Energy Alignment, Values Match, and Burnout Friction—always accompanied by verbatim dated citations from your vault.
